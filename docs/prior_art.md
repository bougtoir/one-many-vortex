## Verdicts on novelty boundary

- What is NOT new (background premises): coexistence of One and Many (Hornung, Versnel, pagan-monotheism volumes, Hindu theism literature); critiques of the mono/poly binary (Masuzawa; "Multi-devotionalism" 2024; "On Counting Gods"); deity-count insufficiency; development of doctrine (Newman); interpretive extension to new problems (usul al-fiqh, responsa, magisterium); retired gods (deus otiosus).
- What plausibly IS new: (a) the Directional Symmetry Test as an explicit reversal audit; (b) the integrated Vortex mechanism with historical novelty as engine; (c) authority-scope release as a jurisdiction operator distinct from secularization and deus otiosus; (d) the semantic-partition reading of deity number with sketch-quantification.
