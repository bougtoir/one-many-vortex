# Phases 3–5 — Case selection, formal model, empirical feasibility

## Cases (4, distinct analytical functions)

1. **Ancient Egypt — Amun-Re syncretism, Amarna, post-Amarna restoration.** Illustrates aggregation (attribute bundling into Amun-Re), radical aggregation (Aten), and re-differentiation with accumulated memory. Sources: Hornung 1996; Assmann 1996, 2008; Baines 2000.
2. **Ancient Israel/Judah — convergence and differentiation of Yahweh.** Illustrates aggregation (El/Baal attributes into Yahweh; Smith's "convergence"), differentiation (Asherah separation; "differentiation" in Smith's terms), and the Mosaic distinction as a boundary operation (Assmann). Sources: Smith 2001, 2002; Assmann 1996.
3. **Greco-Roman / late antiquity — henotheistic and philosophical One.** Illustrates coexistence of One and Many at different registers (theos hypsistos, Neoplatonic One), and shows that "one-god" language need not be monotheism. Sources: Versnel 1990, 2011; Athanassiadi & Frede 1999; Mitchell & Van Nuffelen 2010.
4. **Hindu traditions — Brahman/deva relations and sectarian re-specification.** Illustrates ontological unity coexisting with ritual/narrative plurality, and renewed differentiation (Īśvara re-specification across sectarian lines). Sources: Klostermaier 2007; Cambridge Element on Hindu monotheism (Colas-type scholarship); Sophia 2024 genealogical critique.

Structural illustrations (shorter): Islamic divine names/attributes (Asma' al-Husna) and ijtihad/qiyas as authorized interpretation under novelty; Christian doctrinal development (Newman) and magisterial bioethics (Donum Vitae, Dignitas Personae) as temporal authority extension.

## Formal model (minimal)

- F = {f1..fm} semantic feature space (creation, justice, fertility, ...).
- G = {g1..gn} divine agents; W = (w_ij) feature–agent weights.
- A: partitions/covers of F across G → bundled agent G* (aggregation operator).
- D: G* → re-spread of features across agents (differentiation operator).
- Context C_t = (T_t, Eco_t, Pol_t, K_t, S_t); novelty = C_{t+1} ≠ C_t producing P_{t+1} ∉ stabilized problem space.
- Interpretive distance d(P_{t+1}, N_t); interpretive load L; revision cost C_R; jurisdiction J.
- State vector X_t = (A_eff, B, D, N, I, U, E, R_eff, L, J).
- Optional quantifications (conceptual only): A_eff = exp(−Σ q_g ln q_g) (perplexity); B = Σ q_g² (Herfindahl-type bundling); D = I(F;G)/H(F) (normalized feature-agent association).

## Empirical feasibility (Phase 5)

A fully coded W-matrix over public corpora is **not** defensible within this article's scope: Pulotu covers Austronesian small-scale societies; Seshat codes moralizing-supernatural variables at polity level, not feature–agent partitions; DRH entries are uneven across the needed dimensions; divine-attribute lists (theological dictionaries, Asma' al-Husna) are curated canon, not sampled data. Coding W credibly would require its own multi-coder reliability study.

**Decision: retain the formal model as theory; demonstrate instrument plausibility with historically documented illustrations (the four cases) rather than fabricated quantification. A worked, transparent W-matrix for ONE bounded domain (e.g., Egyptian attested function domains) is offered as an *illustrative example* with coding notes, explicitly labeled illustrative, not evidential.**
