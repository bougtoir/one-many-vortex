## B. Circularity / unfalsifiability

- "Any change can be labeled A or D retrospectively." — §4 concedes deposits bias transitions; §11 lists 9 falsification conditions including "recurrence is rare or absent" and "transformations involve complete semantic replacement." The operators are defined over the W architecture, which anchors them to partition structure rather than vibes. **Adequate.**
- "Historical novelty redescribes any later change." — §6 defines novelty relative to the stabilized repertoire's problem space, not chronology; interpretive distance d(P,N) is the operative variable and is stated to be measurable (§11, arm 2). **Adequate.**
- "Could the theory survive any observation?" — H3's falsifier ("unity consistently predicts interpretive unity under novelty") is a genuinely possible observation. The falsification list also includes "semantic-partition modeling adds no explanatory value" — an honest self-targeting condition. **Adequate.**

## C. Formalism critique

- W matrix, A_eff, B, D, state vector X_t, operators A/D, L, J: all are explicitly framed as *conceptual instruments* (§2, §11 "candidate quantifications ... deliberately conservative"; Limits: "measurement program is future work"). No numeric values are asserted for any construct; Figure 5 carries "values are not empirical estimates."
- Risk: the notation could still *suggest* measurability the paper does not deliver. Mitigation already present: §11 labels quantifications as candidates, not results; Table 2 definitions stay qualitative.
- **Verdict: formalism is load-bearing (it makes the directional-symmetry and state-vector claims precise), not decorative. Retain.**

## D. Historical-case critique

- Cherry-picking: the four cases were "selected for analytical function, not representativeness" (§10, stated verbatim). No case is presented as evidence *for* the model's empirical adequacy; Table 3 column "Analytical function" marks each as illustration / counterexample / plausibility demonstration.
- Oversimplification: transitions are attributed to specialist literature (Hornung, Assmann, Baines, Smith, Versnel, Mitchell & Van Nuffelen, Klostermaier) at each step.
- Emic override: §"Two boundary commitments" forbids reclassifying self-understanding; §10 keeps etic vocabulary flagged.
- **Verdict: claims are at "illustration" level throughout; no "evidence" overreach found. No new cases added.**

## E. Representational inversion critique

- Norm→God is explicitly framed as a *wiring operation that histories can instantiate partially and reversibly*, "not a claim about origins" (§5, verbatim twice). The paper does not assert a universal Norm→God historical sequence.
- God→Norm presupposes a genealogy: no — it requires only that *within* a functioning representation the warrant direction can flip; the paper frames inversion as change in justificatory relation, which is exactly the hostile reviewer's preferred reframing.
- **Verdict: manuscript already avoids universal-origin claims. No fix needed.**

## F. Authority-scope release critique (mandatory)

- Is it secularization? No — §9: sacred significance persists; only case-specific regulatory jurisdiction is relinquished. Casanova's privatization addressed directly (release can leave public potency intact).
- Is it routinization? Routinization transfers charisma into office; release removes a *function's* routing through the authority, not the authority's charisma. Distinction holds as stated.
- Is it institutional differentiation? Differentiation is a macro-social process between institutions; release is defined at the jurisdiction level inside a tradition. Distinction stated in §9/Table 1.
- Is it deus otiosus? §9: the deus otiosus retires from the cosmos; release is narrower and institutional, with worship/sacred centrality persisting.
- Empirically identifiable? H5 + its falsifier ("release cannot be identified in evidence") make the identifiability requirement explicit; §9 gives the observational signature: continued sacrality + jurisdiction-level silence on new problems.
- Sacred-without-jurisdiction: the paper's own required distinction (continued sacred significance vs. continued case-specific regulatory jurisdiction) is stated verbatim in §9 and the Discussion.
- **Verdict: concept survives the attack; the distinguishing observations (jurisdictional silence under novelty while sacrality persists) are already in the text.**

## G. Interpretive multiplication critique

- Does novelty necessarily multiply interpretations? §7 rests the mechanism on underdetermination + demand for binding decisions, not necessity; §9 supplies the alternative branch (release). Silence/consensus are possible where release or where sources happen to determine the mapping — the manuscript's probabilistic H3 ("more likely elicits") and H4 conditions cover this.
- **Verdict: theory is conditional, not deterministic. Adequate.**

## H. Violence / exclusion critique

- Discussion paragraph on sacred values is explicitly conditional ("may, under some conditions, raise the probability ... not necessities, and no violence is attributed to monotheism as such"). No deterministic monotheism→violence chain remains.
- **Verdict: PASS.**

## I. Teleology critique

- The model does not invert Many→One=progress into One→Many=progress: §4 states trajectories revisit regions "under changed constraints" with no rank order; Paths A/B in §9 are contrasted "without ranking"; the Conclusion ("Neither One nor Many is the endpoint") is anti-teleological.
- **Verdict: PASS.**

## J. Reviewer decision

- **Strongest reject argument:** the apparatus is unmeasured; a referee who demands operationalized constructs now rather than as an agenda could call the formalism promissory. The paper's own defense (candidate quantifications + a concrete three-arm agenda + falsification conditions) is honest but does not eliminate the promissory character.
- **Strongest accept argument:** the paper does the thing MTSR exists for — it converts a historiographic pathology (directional vocabulary without an audit) into an explicit instrument, integrates four defensible mechanisms into one architecture, and states falsification conditions precise enough to kill it.
- **Unresolved material weakness:** none requiring scientific revision. The remaining exposure is the standard theory-paper exposure, not a flaw.
